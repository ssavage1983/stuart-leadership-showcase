# Purpose

Filter, structure, and refine career experience into one of the three supported portfolio CV variants on stuart-leadership-showcase.lovable.app.

# Execution Rules

1. **Select Perspective:**
   - **Master Executive CV:** Comprehensive 4-page equivalent detailing full operational impact metrics and complete role histories across all chapters.
   - **Executive Operations CV:** Streamlined 2-page equivalent focused on P&L accountability, supply chain management, digital transformation, and commercial project delivery.
   - **Rail-Optimised CV:** Streamlined 2-page equivalent focused on safety-critical compliance, public safety, crowd flow, risk mitigation, and passenger operations.
2. **Corporate Boundary:** Ensure all historic founder roles (SAVAGELDN, Stuart Savage Landscaping, The Moonlight Garden Design Co.) state only that the remaining businesses closed in May 2026 after health and trading pressures made continued operation unsustainable. Do not claim a planned or solvent wind-down, or that every client or supplier commitment was fulfilled.
3. **Formatting & Localization:** Format dates as DD/MM/YYYY, use sentence case for interactive buttons/tabs, and enforce strict UK English.
