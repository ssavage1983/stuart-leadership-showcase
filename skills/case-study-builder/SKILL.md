# Purpose
Transform unstructured operational project notes into high-impact executive case study components.

# Execution Rules
1. **Asset Framing:** Frame all historical capital works strictly as "Structural Asset Delivery", "Site Logistics Management", or "Infrastructure Operations".
2. **Localization:** Use strict UK English spelling throughout (e.g., programme, modelling, organisation).
3. **Case Study Structure:** Format the final modal/card using these four sections:
   - **Executive Summary:** 2-sentence overview of the operational mandate.
   - **Challenge & Scope:** Key logistical, regulatory, or operational constraints.
   - **Operational Strategy:** Actions taken, risk mitigation, and stakeholder coordination.
   - **Quantifiable Outcomes:** Concrete metrics (P&L impact, delivery timelines, safety compliance).
