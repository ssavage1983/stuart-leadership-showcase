# STAR Story Builder

Use this skill to convert operational leadership achievements into structured STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

## Guidance Rules

1. Focus on transferable commercial leadership, operations, and stakeholder management skills.
2. Structure output strictly into four sections:
   - **Situation:** Set the operational context concisely.
   - **Task:** Define the clear goal or responsibility.
   - **Action:** Highlight specific leadership decisions and tools used.
   - **Result:** Quantify commercial metrics, cost savings, or efficiency gains.
